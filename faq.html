Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about rooms near Temple West Nada.

How far is the lodge from the temple West Entrance / West Nada?

Sree Ayyappan Rooms is 80 meters from the Temple West Entrance (West Nada), less than a 1-minute walk for pilgrims and families.

Is it safe for solo female travelers and families?

Yes. We are located near the Police Check Point. The setting is calm and suitable for families and women travelers.

Are pure vegetarian restaurants available nearby?

Yes. Options within walking distance include Suda Suda Idli, Sri Udupi Hotel And Lodge, Venkadesa Bhavan, SREE Punjabi Dhabha, Hotel Gowri Krishna, and Hotel Annapoorna.

Do you provide hot water and drinking water?

Yes. Guests have pure drinking water and continuous hot water, plus 24/7 room service.

Can we check in early morning or late at night?

Yes. We support early morning and late-night check-in, offer luggage storage for early arrivals, and can share guidance on temple timing and darshan.
